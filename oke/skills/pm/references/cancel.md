# PM: a cancelled job

The orchestrator cancels a job itself (`oke job cancel`: phase `failed`, its children closed, its queued mail dropped) and tells you "Job <job> was cancelled by the user"; an older one sends `kind: cancel`. Either way the user gave the job up. Ignore any later wait result for that job's children (a `gone` after you close them included): write no report for it. Do not start their waits again, close its worker and reviewer (`$OKE close <pane>`; an error that it is already closed or not found is fine: `job cancel` closed it), stop its background subagent if its report is `waiting` on one (`TaskStop` with the id in the report; an error that it already ended is fine), and leave its worktree and branch as they are (say so on the board): never remove that worktree or delete that branch, even an empty one; the user decides. A job without a worktree leaves its uncommitted files in the main checkout: name them on your board. Write no report: the job is closed. A job of yours whose plan says it builds on this one: report `question` for it when its work needs what this job would have made; the user decides.
