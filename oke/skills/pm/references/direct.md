# PM: direct job

`direct: true` in the output of `$OKE job path` and in `$OKE status` under `jobs`: the job's path is a folder outside git, and the files change in place. Git repositories below it, and links that lead out of it, are not this job's: the job never writes there (say so in the worker's 범위; OKe refuses such an Edit or Write, not a Bash write). Plan and execute as usual, with these differences. No worktree, and `commit: no` for the worker. Skip the git steps (`job tree`, `add`, `commit`, `status`). The reviewer's assignment has no `commit:` or `tree:`; its 이번 지시 lists the files the worker reported as changed or new, to read whole. On `verdict: pass` close the worker and reviewer (`$OKE close <pane>`, as merge step 7 does) and report `done` (files changed, the worker's 검증 출력): there is no merge, and `$OKE job merge` refuses one.
